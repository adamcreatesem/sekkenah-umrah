    About us

    An Ethiopian office, on Ethiopian roads.

    Sekkenah is a tour operator at Jemo Michael in Addis Ababa. We run routes across the country — the Rift Valley lakes, the historic north, the Omo, Bale and the Danakil — with our own cars, our own drivers and guides we have travelled with.

    The work is small on purpose. One office answers the phone, the same guide stays with you for the whole route, and nothing is handed to a number you have never called.
